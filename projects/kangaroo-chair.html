Project

Kangaroo Chair

The Kangaroo Chair is a hospital chair that helps mothers of preterm babies in Uganda keep up skin-to-skin care.

Made by
Emile Waterkeyn, Merlijn Lewerissa
Client
Self-initiated
With
Design Without Borders
Year
2022
Type
Product design

Kangaroo Mother Care, skin-to-skin contact for at least eight hours a day, improves the survival of preterm babies by up to 40 percent and needs no medical equipment, yet it is rarely practised in Uganda. With Design Without Borders, Emile and Merlijn collected data in three hospitals in Kampala, ran focus groups with mothers and sorted every barrier they found into six categories.

One barrier is the ward itself: small rooms furnished with hospital beds donated from the Global North, not designed for this kind of care. The chair supports a mother both sitting and lying down, and fits a ward that is already short of space. It was prototyped with local craftsmen, tested in hospitals and well received by mothers. Design Without Borders is now raising funds for local production.
