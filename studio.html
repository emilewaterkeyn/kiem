Studio

Kiem Studio is a design and research studio for circular and ecological impact, run by Emile Waterkeyn in Brussels and Merlijn Lewerissa in Rotterdam.

Every part of the system we live in is a decision that has hardened into habit. How we take, make, use and discard. Transitions begin when those decisions become visible again and therefore open to change. A circular world is not one that wastes less, it is one in which human making is understood as part of a larger living fabric, accountable to what it draws on and what it leaves behind. Design, for us, is a way of thinking within systems and imagining how they could be otherwise.

We stand for less waste, room for nature, and design made with the people it serves.

What we do

Products
Objects and services designed with the people who use them, from a repair system for surf schools to a hospital chair. Tides, Kangaroo Chair, Readme, Bako, Vertical Farming
Spaces
Exhibitions, pavilions and installations that let people walk through an idea. Reharvest, Material Matters
Research
Field research with the people inside a system, workshops with makers and artists, publications and knowledge sessions, and observational documentary film as a research tool. Protein Transition, Material Matters, YIIYA

What we are working on

Materials and residual streams
What a system throws away as the start of something new.
Repair and reuse
Products and services that keep things in use for longer.
Design with nature
Materials that grow, and products and places that leave room for the ecosystems around them.

We are looking for clients and partners who want to try something new and test it in practice.

Who we are

We studied Industrial Design Engineering and Integrated Product Design at TU Delft in the same years, and have made projects together since 2022.

Emile Waterkeyn

Brussels

Designer working between systemic and speculative design, on the bio-economy and circular systems. Emile studied Industrial Design Engineering and Integrated Product Design at TU Delft, did mycelium research at Spore and has led innovation projects as Living Lab project manager at Stichting Innovatie Glastuinbouw Nederland. He founded the textile printing company De Print Bar, which is still running, and makes observational documentaries: YIIYA, and Carrying Steel, about the supply chain behind a fork.

Merlijn Lewerissa

Rotterdam

Product designer and maker. Merlijn studied Industrial Design Engineering and Integrated Product Design at TU Delft and spent an exchange term at ArtCenter College of Design in Pasadena. His graduation project Tides received funding from Sportinnovator and ZonMw to continue after graduating. He teaches furniture making at Buurman Rotterdam, a collective that works with circular wood, and designed passenger experiences at Royal Schiphol Group, including a concept for the grass cut around the runways.
